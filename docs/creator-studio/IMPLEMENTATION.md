# Tryumph Creator Studio — implementation and founder handoff

November 4, 2026. Development branch `codex/tryumph-january-launch`. This release is not production deployment approval. No hosted SQL, live charge, email, transfer, refund, merge, DNS change or provider activation was executed.

## Implemented and connected

- Dedicated responsive Creator Studio with all twelve requested sections plus Marketing & Spotlight. Password sign-in reads the authenticated profile before choosing trainer/member landing. Studio independently checks the persisted role and remounts business state on identity changes. The member-view switch changes presentation/navigation only, never the role or identity.
- Date-scoped business analytics: actual follower events, current totals, authorized playback starts, Trybe membership/growth, active paying subscribers, five revenue sources, net trainer earnings and related pending/completed Connect transfers. 7/30/90/custom windows; a maximum one-year custom range. Transfers are not represented as verified bank payouts. Historical follower events/views are not invented. Watch time is explicitly unavailable rather than estimated from an open tab.
- Private Work With Me form and partnership inbox: authenticated sender, database validation, advisory-lock rate limit, ownership/RLS, block controls, five inquiry states, preference-aware deduplicated notices. It never creates a friend request, friendship or message permission. Existing manual trainer inquiry messaging policy remains separate.
- Optional memberships recommend $29.99, use admin-configurable bounds/recommendation, and preserve existing agreed subscription prices. The existing real-invoice, actual-fee, 90/10 Stripe billing/Connect/refund/dispute architecture remains in use. Owner/runtime gates remain separate and disabled until staging/provider acceptance.
- A current subscription includes this trainer’s scheduled classes and spontaneous subscriber-only lives without a $5 checkout, wallet debit, live earning or extra revenue split. Non-subscribers retain $5/attendance-backed tokens. Subscription attendance has a separate ledger; an expired subscription cannot silently spend a token. An explicit rebooking can resume the normal paid attendance flow. If an open class-specific $5 top-up settles alongside a subscription covering the class, its unused token is held and the existing durable refund workflow queues a real refund; confirmed refund status is never fabricated. Both settlement event orders are reconciled. Legitimately spent tokens and unrelated bulk wallet purchases are preserved. Session access type is immutable; frontend writes cannot claim broadcasting started. Provider-signed attendance enables subscription rooms and sends opted-in announcements. Existing participant tokens remain attendee-only. Access deadlines are capped to the real paid period. Connected rooms are reauthorized every existing five-minute maintenance cycle; JWT expiry alone is not described as eviction.
- The latest brief restores five approved videos plus current required verified credentials/CPR/AED for both live types. Existing scheduled classes whose trainer no longer qualifies require review; the migration preserves their rows and financial history. Current eligibility is enforced at scheduling, checkout, token issuance, attendance and room maintenance.
- Existing structured program builder, ordered weeks/days/workouts, synchronized exercise variations/timers, protected PDFs and Bunny media are reused. Free, one-time paid, external purchase and Members Only are supported. Paid editions include a subscription benefit only when explicitly configured. Editions/progress are pinned, independent purchases survive subscription expiration, and subscription access expires/refunds without deleting progress. PDF/offline completion is idempotent; aggregate engagement includes historical editions, starts, average progress, completions and lesson checkpoints. Automatic video completion remains optional and is not enabled; manual completion is available.
- **New hosted PDF/video/mixed sales use 90% trainer / 10% Tryumph after actual settled payment-processing fees, before hosting/infrastructure.** Trainer cents use floor(net × 0.90); Tryumph gets the integer-cent remainder. $50 − $1.75 fees = $48.25 net; trainer $43.42, Tryumph $4.83. Refund/dispute reconciliations retain each order’s frozen rate and idempotent transfer/reversal operations. Historical 95/5 orders remain 95/5. Migration 0066 disables new checkout commercial configuration until the owner records a new 1000-bps terms version; it does not alter historical orders or activate Stripe.
- External affiliate links and independent stores remain 100% trainer earnings. Other splits stay: subscriptions/private 90/10, scheduled paid drop-ins/video-water-break ads 50/50, community feed advertising 100% Tryumph. Free on-demand and free Trybes remain available. Ads, timers and safety exclusions are preserved; no mid-set ad insertion was added.
- Existing content metadata, library upload/review, subscriber/early/public visibility, community posts/photos/polls/stores and booking tools are reused. New workout discussions require authorized media access and appropriate Trybe membership. Trainers can reply and hide/restore comments on their own content with an audit trail. Community comment moderation preserves the original text. Private content comments cannot expose premium content.
- Subscriber live recordings use the distinct protected Bunny library and subscriber audience, remain pending independent review, contain no automatic ads, and fail closed unless protected delivery is verified. Public scheduled recordings retain the existing public-library review pipeline. R2/egress/Bunny behavior still needs provider testing.
- Admin-managed Creator Perks with offer/eligibility/expiry/redemption/referral disclosures, current verified-trainer RLS and credential renewal cards. No fictional active partner deals are seeded. Tryumph School has audience-aware search, instructions, screenshots/alt text, optional approved videos, publication and updated dates. Fourteen creator and ten member topics are seeded only as unpublished drafts. Admin editors and a member School route are connected.
- Existing notice center now includes creator updates and explicit subscriber-live opt-in. Real follower, invoice, purchase, review, message, partnership, community/workout comment, completion, transfer, scheduled-live and spontaneous-live events reuse deduplicated notices and existing credential/session reminders.

## What Members Are Looking For

Trending, Content Gaps and My Audience are connected to database aggregation. Existing on-demand controls, program search/categories and live focus filters issue asynchronous telemetry RPCs after discovery, without blocking results. Server-normalized allowlisted fitness terms, non-sensitive goal/category/equipment/duration/difficulty selections and server-counted approved public supply are retained. Repeating the same selection counts once per member/day/source, and reports count distinct members across the period.

Arbitrary free text, trainer names, contact information, diagnoses, clinical/life-stage/accessibility labels and stored personal health preferences are excluded. These filters still work for browsing. The tracker is not a source of clinical demand analytics. It stores a salted pseudonymous key, not member IDs, names, email, IP or raw search text; it remains personal data and needs the published privacy/retention review. There are at most twenty distinct daily selections per account. No raw events or keys are client-readable, even to trainers. Service maintenance deletes events older than 90 days. The private salt is never returned to clients.

Reports use fixed complete-day 7/30/90 UTC windows. At least twenty distinct members in a report cohort and ten per topic are required. Zero/low-result counts are separately suppressed below ten. My Audience uses current followers and authorized playback engagement; small groups get no results. No individual history is exposed. Supply is the public approved inventory observed at the event, not a prediction or guaranteed current inventory. Historical demand is never fabricated. A database administrator with service access remains able to inspect pseudonymous events; trainer-facing RLS and server aggregation do not claim irreversible anonymization.
